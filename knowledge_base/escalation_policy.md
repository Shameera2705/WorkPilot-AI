# Support Escalation Guide

- Critical production outages should be acknowledged immediately and escalated to the on-call engineer.
- High-priority payment or booking failures should be assigned an owner and investigated promptly.
- Any ticket open longer than 24 hours should receive an owner review and a customer update.
- Customer-facing updates must state what is known, what is being investigated, and when the next update is expected.
- Never promise a resolution time unless the responsible team has confirmed it.
